4. Tech Jargon Explained

Short definitions to make sure you understand the marketing terms behind each laptop choice.

NPU / TOPS

Neural Processing Units are specialized chips used for AI tasks. Higher TOPS means faster on-device model performance.

TGP

Total Graphics Power describes the GPU power budget. More TGP typically means stronger sustained gaming performance.

LPDDR5X

Low-power DDR5X memory offers faster bandwidth and better energy efficiency, especially for premium ultrabooks.

Single Slot SSD

Laptops with only one SSD slot are harder to upgrade later. Prefer dual-slot designs when long-term storage flexibility matters.
